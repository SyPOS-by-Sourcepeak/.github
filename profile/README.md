# ZeloPOS

**ZeloPOS by ZeloOS** is a modern, modular point of sale and inventory management platform built to help businesses keep sales moving while staying on top of their operations and inventory.

This repository houses organization-level documentation, shared configuration, and community guidance for the ZeloPOS ecosystem.

## What We Do

* Deliver a unified POS experience that works online and offline
* Manage products, inventory, suppliers, and purchase orders
* Provide tools for sales, operations, and day-to-day business management
* Surface analytics and insights to support better business decisions
* Offer extensible integrations for payments, accounting, and ecommerce

## Product Pillars

* **Business Ready**: Fast, intuitive interfaces and efficient workflows designed for real-world business operations, from daily sales to payments and receipts.

* **Inventory Intelligence**: SKU-level insights, stock tracking, reorder management, low-stock alerts, and tools for managing inventory across stores and operations.

* **Open & Extensible**: APIs, integration hooks, and shared components that allow ZeloPOS to connect with the tools and systems businesses already use.

* **Insight Driven**: Dashboards, reports, and operational insights that turn sales and inventory data into actionable information.

## ZeloPOS Editions

ZeloPOS is designed for different types of businesses through specialized editions:

* **ZeloPOS Retail**
* **ZeloPOS Restaurant**
* **ZeloPOS Hotel**
* **ZeloPOS Services**

Each edition is tailored to the workflows and operational requirements of its respective business type while sharing the same core ZeloPOS platform.

## Projects in the Organization

The ZeloPOS organization consists of several repositories that work together to build and operate the platform:

* `sypos-api`: Core backend services, business logic, inventory systems, and integrations
* `sypos-web`: Web-based POS terminal and management console
* `sypos-devops`: Infrastructure as code, deployment templates, and monitoring

> Note: Repository names may retain legacy `sypos` identifiers for technical compatibility. The user-facing product and organization brand is **ZeloPOS**.

## About ZeloOS

**ZeloOS** is the technology company behind ZeloPOS.

ZeloOS builds software products and technology solutions, with ZeloPOS being its point of sale and business management platform.
